## Working rules

- Design conversation produces proposals; Work reconciles them; this repository holds canonical approved design; engineering implements bounded work packages.
- Preserve the full future service vision while delivering the assessment vertical slice first.
- Customers manage marketing outcomes, not prompts, models, or agent tools.
- Keep observed facts, estimates, recommendations, and measured results distinct.
- Missing data is unknown, not zero. Recommendations need evidence or an explicit hypothesis label.
- Engineering may choose reversible implementation details within approved scope. Material product gaps require a recorded design decision.
- Do not insert credentials, customer exports, or private operational data into this design repository.
