# Customer Experience

Status: source-backed requirements; screen grouping below is a proposed presentation, not an approved wireframe. Sources: H2, H3, H5.

## Intake

Required for assessment: public domain, business description, desired customers/business, priority products/services, and target geography. The broader business profile also captures business name, products/services offered, important markets, and optional marketing/ad budget and connected properties. Business name may be confirmed during intake; it must not be a prerequisite for knowing marketing terminology.

Optional context: current priorities, known competitors, Google Ads account, Business Profile, Search Console, Analytics, and other authorized properties. A keyword list is not required. If website observations contradict submitted goals, preserve the customer's stated intent and surface the mismatch.

## First journey

1. Describe the business, its domain, geography, and desired work in ordinary language.
2. Review what MOMS understands about the goals and priority offerings.
3. Run the public assessment and see progress or a comprehensible failure/coverage message.
4. Read current state and recommended work, including why each action matters.
5. Inspect evidence, cost/effort estimates, dependencies, confidence, and measurement window.
6. See the recommended next decisions and revisit the saved report and baseline.

Screen proposals: Business & Goals, Assessment Progress, Report, and Recommended Work detail. Final screen layout and end-user account provisioning are open decisions. The design can be validated through an authorized operator-assisted implementation without inventing a production self-service access policy.

## Later portal views

Business Goals; Current Visibility; Opportunities; Recommended Work; Work in Progress; Decisions / Approvals Needed; Advertising; Results; Marketing Activity; Reports.

Advertising answers: what are we spending, what are we getting, where is money being wasted, what could we target, what could additional budget purchase, and what needs approval?

## Presentation rules

Use plain business language. Separate observed facts from estimates. State unavailable sources and incomplete coverage. Do not represent a draft as published, an approved change as executed, traffic as revenue, or activation as proven business benefit. Internal model selection, prompts, and tools are not customer workflow requirements.

See [report contract](report-contract.md), [work items](marketing-work-item.md), and [benefit model](benefit-and-time-model.md).

## Expanded agency and customer journeys

Add Strategy, AI Visibility, Content Calendar, Content Review, Authority Work and Proposals as applicable capabilities. Agency operators receive a customer portfolio with attention and delivery status; customer users see only their permitted business context. The [portal design](agency-portal-and-reporting.md) owns branding and delivery, while [proposals](proposals-and-onboarding.md) owns prospect-to-onboarding behavior. Exact navigation layouts remain proposals. Customers can start with business goals; advanced prompt/keyword controls are optional.
