# Sandy Getting Started: Step 2 — Comprehensive Online Presence Audit
Status: user-confirmed canonical deep-dive checklist, 2026-10-17. Design only; not implemented.

## Purpose
Expand each Step 1 discovery into evidence-backed assessment, including owned resources, third-party references and missing capabilities. Publicly observable checks first; restricted analytics/account details require authorized connections. A missing website is an assessed gap, not a failed onboarding.

## Canonical audit categories and checks
1. **Company identity and branding:** legal/trade names, subsidiaries/divisions/locations, NAP consistency, logos, descriptions and conflicting identities.
2. **Websites:** associated sites, availability, mobile usability, performance, HTTPS, broken links, navigation, accessibility, content and calls to action.
3. **Domains and infrastructure:** domains/subdomains, public registration, DNS, redirects, certificates, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, observable configuration risks.
4. **Business directories:** Google/Bing/Apple/Yelp/industry listings, duplicate records, names/addresses/phones, categories, hours and missing listings.
5. **Social profiles:** official/unofficial identities, profile completeness, branding, posting, public engagement and abandoned accounts.
6. **Social references:** mentions, tags, discussions, sentiment, reputation concerns and influential references.
7. **Search and SEO:** branded results, indexing, metadata, technical SEO, keywords, backlinks, local visibility and observable AI-search references.
8. **Reviews and reputation:** review sites, counts/ratings, sentiment, responses, recurring complaints and trends.
9. **Advertising:** publicly visible campaigns/ad libraries, messaging, landing pages, opportunities and gated private performance data.
10. **Analytics and tracking:** observable analytics/tag/pixel technologies, conversion paths, consent/privacy signals and authorized account verification.
11. **Content:** articles, press, video, podcasts, freshness, quality, outdated and missing topics.
12. **Third-party affiliations:** partners, vendors, parent/sister sites, affiliates, media mentions, descriptions and broken references.
13. **Industry profiles:** associations, chambers, certifications, accreditations, professional and industry directories.
14. **Engagement channels:** forms, contact paths, chat, scheduling, lead generation, journey issues and permissioned functional tests.
15. **Commerce and marketplaces:** storefronts, catalogs, listings, prices, availability, ratings, inquiry/purchase experiences and inconsistencies.
16. **Competitors:** relevant competitors and comparative sites, SEO, listings, social, reviews, visible ads and opportunities.
17. **Digital risk, breach exposure and dark web intelligence:** detailed checklist in [Category 17](digital-risk-category-17.md).

## Standard evaluation record
For each applicable check record: item reference, check identifier, status (Pass / Issue / Opportunity / Not Applicable / Unable to Verify), evidence/provenance/date, severity, recommendation, execution class (Automated / Approval Required / Manual / Strategic), responsible party and tracking status. Never treat inaccessible data as zero or a pass. Findings link to existing [Marketing Work Items](../marketing-work-item.md), not a second work-item model.

## Execution and completion
Audit all applicable confirmed items, identify missing capabilities, avoid duplicated technical checks for shared resources, document coverage and unknowns, preserve historical observations, and generate:
- Comprehensive Online Presence Audit Report
- Online Presence Health Dashboard
- Action and Opportunity Register

This design extends existing [SEO/site assessment](../seo-site-assessment.md) and [evidence model](../domain-model.md); it does not expand EWP-001 or authorize external writes.
