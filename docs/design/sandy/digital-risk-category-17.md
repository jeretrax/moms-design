# Sandy Digital Risk Discovery and Category 17 Deep Dive
Status: user-confirmed inclusion in Steps 1 and 2, 2026-10-17; provider and execution specifics proposed.

## Step 1: discovery
Search legitimate public and licensed intelligence for references to known company identities/domains: breach and exposed email reports, ransomware/extortion claims, defacement or malware reports, lookalike/phishing domains, reported leaks, and underground/dark-web mentions. Catalog source, URL/provider identifier, first observed, discovered date, affected identity, evidence summary, confidence, source reliability and verification status. Confirm the *identity association* with the reviewer, not the truth of an allegation.

## Step 2: deep-dive checklist
- [ ] **Breach exposure:** affected domains, reported dates and exposure types; historical versus possible active risk; recurring incidents.
- [ ] **Credential exposure:** reported employee-email, infostealer, password/session-token exposure indicators; authorized security escalation.
- [ ] **Ransomware/extortion:** public intelligence and claimed victim listings, timeline, independent corroboration, reputational impact.
- [ ] **Website compromise:** malicious URL reputation, defacement, malware, phishing, suspicious redirects and observable warnings.
- [ ] **Brand impersonation:** lookalike domains, fraudulent sites/social accounts, phishing campaigns, potential takedown/referral.
- [ ] **Underground mentions:** lawful provider intelligence concerning forums, marketplaces and leak claims; reliability and relevance.
- [ ] **Information leakage:** reported exposed documents and sensitive information; affected assets and authorized investigation.
- [ ] **Risk recommendations:** credibility, severity, affected identity, business impact, response owner, execution method and follow-up.

## Boundaries
Do not obtain or retain stolen passwords, tokens or leaked documents unnecessarily. Do not purchase stolen information or participate in criminal marketplaces. A reported breach or criminal claim is not proof of company compromise. Sandy detects, records, monitors and recommends; authorized security responders or ITOMS handle remediation. Promptly escalate credible critical findings when detected, subject to configured channels; seven-day default scans do not imply real-time coverage.

## Provider evaluation
Candidates to assess separately: Have I Been Pwned, Flare, VirusTotal, Google Web Risk, ransomware intelligence feeds, urlscan.io. Validate API terms, customer domain verification, commercial licensing, permissions, costs and privacy before integration. No provider or subscription is selected here.
