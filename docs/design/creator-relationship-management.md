# Creator Relationship Management

Status: canonical owner of creator lifecycle, extending the existing InfluencerEngagement in R-028 with R-039. Reference: [Brandwatch Influence](https://www.brandwatch.com/products/influence/).

Add creator discovery/filtering, a reviewed roster, campaign relationships, briefs/contracts, content approvals, deliverable tracking and performance reports. Support scoped creator collaboration and authorized payment-provider references. Discovery indicators can include declared topics, prior brand activity and available audience/engagement evidence.

## MOMS identity and control model

CreatorProfile links verified external accounts, authorized contact references and source-dated discovery observations. Keep uncertain matches separate. Creator audiences are provider estimates with method/date, not verified individual contacts or guaranteed reach. Roster membership is scoped to customer/agency grants; contracts, correspondence and negotiated rates do not become universally shared agency data.

Existing InfluencerEngagement owns the campaign-specific relationship, scope, rights, approved compensation and acceptance. Content/Media revisions and WorkAssignments own deliverables. The shared inbox/outreach transport owns communications; creator portal is a scoped view of the existing agency portal. Do not build a second CRM, media store or payment ledger.

Record permitted use, expiry, channel, paid amplification rights and approval revision. Reusing an asset or boosting a creator post rechecks those grants; creator submission alone is not permission. Payout proposals link accepted deliverables and approved commercial terms to existing cost/payment records. Use provider references instead of raw payment credentials; authorization, provider eligibility, currency rules and idempotent reconciliation precede payment. Disputes remain visible and never trigger duplicate payment retries.

## Acceptance

CR-01: Unverified creator account matches cannot merge identities or send outreach.

CR-02: Expired or channel-incompatible rights block reuse/boosting even for previously approved content.

CR-03: A repeated acceptance/payment callback cannot duplicate compensation, and creators see only their authorized engagements.
