# Authority and Outreach

Status: user-requested capability expansion, 2026-09-19. Requirement R-022. Reference: [Adaptify backlinks](https://adaptify.ai/features/backlinks).

Expand authority opportunities into prospect research, expert profiles, pitch drafting, approved outreach, response handling and placement tracking. Evaluate relevant publications, journalist requests and guest contributions. Let customers review proposed pitches and placements, flag unsuitable results, and request corrective work. Track link destination, publisher, target page, placement date and later availability; report evidence rather than guaranteeing a number or quality of earned links.

## MOMS authority boundary

Expert identities and claims require customer confirmation. Separate sender authorization, recipient/channel permission, message approval and any third-party purchase. Resolve recipients before delivery. Suppression, opt-out handling, frequency limits and revoked sender authority must be deterministic; policy and service-provider selection remain D-016. This design change sends no outreach.

Track sourced quality indicators with dates; a third-party authority metric is not a ranking guarantee. Record rel attributes, redirects, disclosure/sponsorship metadata and unavailable observations. Prohibit deceptive personas, fabricated expertise and spam. Exclude undisclosed paid ranking manipulation; any sponsorship or paid placement needs explicit policy review and budget authorization. Replacement requests create work items and do not imply a replacement guarantee or preapproved spend.

## Acceptance

AO-01: No pitch sends without matching sender, recipient policy and content authorization.

AO-02: Suppressed recipients and revoked accounts are blocked even for queued work.

AO-03: A lost or changed placement records new evidence and opens linked corrective work without rewriting the original placement history.
