## Scope and execution

EWP-001, M1, M2 Google Ads read/recommend and M3 measurement retain their existing boundaries. The additions can be packaged after M1 according to dependencies; they are not a silent expansion of M1 or a replacement for Google Ads. No feature implies automatic write authorization.

Recommended next package candidates are strategy/visibility read capabilities, content drafting, then separately gated publishing, outreach, reporting delivery and commerce. These are planning candidates, not released EWPs. Create bounded packages with the owner-document acceptance IDs before implementation. Reuse customer, goal, evidence, work item, approval, execution and measurement records throughout.

## End-to-end design acceptance

EX-01: An observed gap links to an existing goal and work item; approved content revision, external execution, verified evidence and later measurement retain the same lineage.

EX-02: Prospect conversion reuses its assessed customer/property context and cannot bypass action controls.

EX-03: No client can retrieve another client's content, credentials, prompts, reports or commercial documents through portfolio, custom-domain or sharing routes.
