# MOMS Product Design Handoff

## Product Name

**MOMS - Marketing Operations Management System**

Repository: `moms-design`

## Product Vision

MOMS is a subscription marketing service that uses AI agents,
deterministic automation, marketing data sources, and human approvals to
continuously improve a customer's online visibility and customer
acquisition.

The customer does not need to understand AI agents, SEO platforms,
keyword tools, APIs, or advertising consoles. The service should
translate the customer's ordinary business objectives into marketing
work.

A customer should be able to say things such as:

-   We want more commercial HVAC replacement jobs in northwest Houston.
-   We want to sell more of these three products.
-   We want more calls from businesses rather than residential
    customers.
-   We want to become more visible when people are searching for the
    services we provide.

MOMS converts that intent into research, opportunities, recommendations,
work, execution, and measurement.

## Core Operating Loop

1.  Understand the business.
2.  Understand what business the customer wants to acquire.
3.  Inspect the customer's public web presence.
4.  Discover how prospective customers actually search for those needs.
5.  Analyze search results and competing businesses.
6.  Identify organic, paid, local, content, authority, and presence
    opportunities.
7.  Create prioritized Marketing Work Items.
8.  Explain expected benefit, cost, effort, risk, and expected time to
    measurable benefit.
9.  Request approval when required.
10. Execute work that is authorized for automation.
11. Measure results.
12. Reassess priorities and repeat.

## Customer Intake

The customer should not be required to know marketing terminology or
provide a keyword list.

Minimum intake:

-   Company/business name
-   Public website/domain
-   Primary service geography
-   Description of the business
-   Products/services offered
-   Products/services currently prioritized
-   Types of customers or business the company wants more of
-   Important geographic markets
-   Approximate marketing/ad budget, if applicable
-   Existing Google Ads account, if applicable
-   Existing Google Business Profile, Search Console, Analytics, and
    other connected properties

The system generates candidate keywords, topics, search intents,
competitors, and opportunities from this information.

## Major Capability Areas

### 1. SEO and Website Optimization

Analyze the public domain and produce a prioritized assessment covering
areas such as:

-   Search visibility
-   Search intent
-   Candidate keywords and topics
-   Current rankings where available
-   Competitor visibility
-   Site structure
-   Technical SEO
-   Indexability
-   metadata
-   page titles and descriptions
-   headings
-   internal linking
-   content gaps
-   service pages
-   location pages
-   structured data opportunities
-   page performance
-   mobile experience
-   local search signals
-   Google Business Profile opportunities
-   reviews and reputation signals
-   backlinks and authority opportunities
-   AI/search answer visibility where measurable

The result is not merely an SEO score. It is a work plan.

### 2. Paid Search / Google Ads

MOMS should integrate with Google Ads.

Initial mode:

-   Read campaign configuration and performance.
-   Analyze keywords and search terms.
-   Analyze spend and pacing.
-   Identify wasted or low-value spend.
-   Discover candidate paid keywords.
-   Obtain or calculate available CPC and traffic estimates.
-   Recommend campaign structure.
-   Recommend budgets.
-   Recommend negative keywords.
-   Recommend landing pages and improvements.
-   Estimate the practical amount of paid visibility that a proposed
    budget can purchase.

Later controlled mode:

**recommend -\> approve -\> execute -\> measure**

MOMS may make authorized Google Ads changes only within defined
permissions and spending controls.

### 3. Benefit and Time-to-Result

Every material recommendation should communicate:

-   What we propose doing
-   Why
-   Expected benefit
-   Whether benefit is immediate, near-term, or longer-term
-   Approximate time before useful measurement is possible
-   Estimated cost
-   Estimated effort
-   Dependencies
-   Confidence/evidence
-   Measurement method

Paid search can produce traffic shortly after a campaign becomes active,
but results and conversions still require measurement.

Organic ranking improvements should never be presented as guaranteed
rankings or guaranteed dates.

### 4. Presence and Authority

MOMS should identify legitimate opportunities outside the customer's own
website, including:

-   Relevant business directories
-   Industry directories
-   Local citations
-   LinkedIn
-   Social platforms
-   Reddit communities
-   Forums
-   trade associations
-   partner/vendor directories
-   review platforms
-   local organizations
-   publications
-   sponsorship or community opportunities
-   earned-link opportunities

The system should distinguish legitimate participation from spam.

MOMS should not autonomously create deceptive personas, spam
communities, or mass-post promotional content merely to manufacture
links.

### 5. Content

MOMS should eventually support:

-   Content opportunity discovery
-   Content briefs
-   Service-page drafts
-   Landing pages
-   FAQs
-   articles
-   comparison pages
-   location content
-   ad copy
-   social content
-   campaign content
-   revision recommendations

Publication permissions must be independently controllable.

## Customer Experience

The customer portal should emphasize business decisions rather than
marketing jargon.

Primary views should eventually include:

-   Business Goals
-   Current Visibility
-   Opportunities
-   Recommended Work
-   Work in Progress
-   Decisions / Approvals Needed
-   Advertising
-   Results
-   Marketing Activity
-   Reports

The customer should be able to understand:

**Where are we now? What should we do? What will it cost? What benefit
do we expect? When should we measure it? What happened after we did
it?**

## Product Principle

MOMS is not a dashboard that merely reports marketing data.

Its differentiator is the closed operational loop:

**observe -\> understand -\> recommend -\> approve -\> execute -\>
measure -\> learn**
